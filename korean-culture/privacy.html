1. What this app is

"Introduction to Korean Culture Uploader" ("the app") is a personal command-line tool made by the owner of the YouTube channel 「Introduction to Korean Culture」 (https://www.youtube.com/@IntroductiontoKoreanCulture, channel ID UC3ormVeNltFC2mq0IGRcGhQ) to upload the owner's own videos to that channel. It runs only on the channel owner's PC(s), and its only user is the channel owner (kado79.youtube2@gmail.com). It is not a service that other people sign up for or sign in to, and it has no server.

The owner also runs a separate uploader for another channel, 「보통의 질문」 ("보통의 질문 업로더", with its own privacy policy at https://kado79.github.io/privacy.html). This app is a different app: it has its own Google Cloud project, OAuth client, authorization token and consent record (the OAuth client configuration, token and consent record are in the same .env file as that app's, but kept as this app's own entries under separate names: YT_OVERSEAS_… for this app, YT_DOMESTIC_… for that app), and it does not upload to, read from or reply on that other channel. Upload records of that other app kept in the same folders are only checked for which channel they belong to, so that this app leaves them alone; this app never refreshes, changes or deletes them.

The app is not made or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

This policy describes how the app accesses, uses, stores, and shares Google user data, including data received through YouTube API Services.

When the owner first authorizes the app, the app shows the address of this policy and starts the authorization flow only after the owner agrees to it. The app also checks this agreement before its authorization, upload, publish, comment and reply commands run, and after the effective date of this policy (or, for a second change on the same day, its version) changes, these commands run only after the owner agrees to the new version.
