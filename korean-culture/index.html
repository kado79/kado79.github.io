Introduction to Korean Culture Uploader

A channel-owner-only upload tool made by the owner of the YouTube channel 「Introduction to Korean Culture」 to upload the owner's own videos to that channel.

  • Privacy Policy
  • Channel 「Introduction to Korean Culture」
  • 한국어

What it does

  • When the channel owner runs a command on the owner's PC, it uploads the video file, title, description, tags and privacy setting the owner has reviewed to the 「Introduction to Korean Culture」 channel through the YouTube Data API, and adds the thumbnail and caption track.
  • It checks the privacy status and processing status of uploaded videos and, when the owner chooses "make public when processing finishes", makes the video public after processing.
  • It finds public comments on the owner's videos that the channel has not yet answered, and, when the owner tells it to, posts replies as the channel.
  • The default privacy status is "private"; only the value the owner chooses is used.

Why it needs Google account access

Uploading videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's channel requires authorization from that channel's account, so the app requests two YouTube scopes (youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl). youtube.upload is used to upload videos and thumbnails; youtube.force-ssl is used to upload caption tracks (which requires this scope), to confirm the token belongs to the 「Introduction to Korean Culture」 channel, to check the processing status of uploaded videos, to make a processed video public when the owner chose it (videos.update), and to read comments on the owner's own videos and post replies. Apart from public comments on the owner's own videos, the app does not access subscriber or analytics data or other channels' information. If the authorized account is not the 「Introduction to Korean Culture」 channel, nothing is uploaded. How the received information is used and stored is described in the Privacy Policy.

Who uses it

The only user is the channel owner (kado79.youtube2@gmail.com). It is a command-line tool that runs only on the owner's PC; it is not a service that other people sign up for or sign in to, and it has no server. It is separate from the owner's uploader for the channel 「보통의 질문」 (보통의 질문 업로더) and does not access that channel.

왜 Google 계정 권한이 필요한가

채널 주인의 채널에 영상·썸네일·자막을 올리려면 그 채널 계정의 승인이 필요합니다. 그래서 YouTube 권한 두 가지(youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl)를 요청합니다. 영상·썸네일을 올리는 데는 youtube.upload 를, 자막 트랙을 올리고(이 작업은 이 권한만 받습니다) 토큰이 「Introduction to Korean Culture」 채널의 것인지 확인하고 올린 영상의 처리 상태를 보고, 채널 주인이 고른 경우 처리가 끝난 영상을 공개로 바꾸고(videos.update), 내 채널 영상의 댓글을 읽어 답글을 다는 데는 youtube.force-ssl 을 씁니다. 내 채널 영상의 공개 댓글 말고는 구독자·분석 정보나 다른 채널의 정보에 접근하지 않습니다. 승인한 계정이 「Introduction to Korean Culture」 채널이 아니면 아무것도 올리지 않습니다. 받은 정보를 어떻게 쓰고 저장하는지는 개인정보처리방침에 적었습니다.
